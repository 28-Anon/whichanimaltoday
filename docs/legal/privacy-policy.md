# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [fill in the actual publish date]**

WhichAnimalToday ("the site," "we," "us") is a free daily animal-guessing
game. This policy explains what data is collected when you visit and how
it's used.

## Information we collect

**We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email
address, or any other personal information to play the game.**

- **Game progress (streaks, today's result):** stored locally in your
  browser using `localStorage`. This data never leaves your device and is
  not sent to us or any server we control.
- **Advertising cookies:** this site displays ads served by Google
  AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies
  to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. See "Third
  parties" below for how to control this.
- **Basic server logs:** like virtually every website, our hosting
  providers (Framer, GitHub) may log standard technical information
  (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and
  operational purposes. We do not access or use these logs ourselves.

## Third parties

**Google AdSense.** We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use
cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other
websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting
[Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or
[www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

If you're in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you'll see a consent banner on
first visit asking for your preferences on personalized ads, managed
through Google's consent tools. You can change your choice at any time
using that same banner.

## Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at
children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children. If this site is later determined to attract a significant
child audience, ad settings will be updated to serve non-personalized
ads only to visitors in that category, per Google's requirements for
child-directed content.

## Your choices

- Clear your browser's `localStorage` at any time to reset your local
  game progress and streak.
- Use your browser's cookie settings or the opt-out links above to
  control advertising cookies.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will be
revised. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the
updated policy.
