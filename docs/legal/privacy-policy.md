## 2. What does this policy cover?

This policy covers WhichAnimalToday (this website) only. It doesn't
cover any other site or service you might reach by clicking a link from
here — Wikimedia Commons (where our animal photos come from), Google
AdSense, or anywhere else has its own separate privacy policy, and your
information there follows their rules, not ours.

## 3. What information do we collect?

**We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email
address, or any other personal information to play the game.** There is
nothing to sign up for.

- **Game progress (your streak, today's result):** stored locally in
  your own browser using `localStorage`. This never leaves your device
  and is never sent to us or any server we control. Clearing your
  browser's site data resets it.
- **Advertising cookies:** this site displays, or is intended to
  display, ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may
  use cookies to serve ads based on your visits here and elsewhere. See
  section 4.
- **Basic hosting logs:** like virtually every website, our hosting
  providers (Framer, GitHub) may log standard technical information
  (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and
  operational purposes. We don't access, analyze, or otherwise use
  these logs ourselves.

## 4. How does this site use cookies and advertising?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored in your browser. This site's
own code doesn't set any cookies of its own — the only cookies involved
come from Google AdSense (once it's active), used to serve and measure
ads.

If you're in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you'll see a consent banner on
your first visit asking for your preference on personalized ads —
that's managed directly through Google's own consent tools, not
anything custom built into this site. You can change your choice at any
time using that same banner.

To opt out of personalized advertising more broadly, visit
[Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or
[www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## 5. What about children's privacy?

This site is intended for a general audience and isn't specifically
directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal
information from children, largely because we don't collect personal
information from anyone — there's no account or sign-up on this site at
all. If this site is later found to attract a significant child
audience, its ad settings will be updated to serve only
non-personalized ads to visitors in that category, per Google's
requirements for child-directed content.

## 6. What are your choices?

- Clear your browser's site data at any time to reset your local game
  progress and streak — nothing to request from us, since we never
  hold a copy of it.
- Use your browser's cookie settings, or the opt-out links in section
  4, to control advertising cookies.

Because we don't collect or hold any personal information ourselves,
there's no account to delete and no personal data of yours for us to
correct or hand over — there's simply nothing on our end to ask for.
